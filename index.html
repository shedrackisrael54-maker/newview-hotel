Welcome

Sapele Road address. G.R.A. quiet.

We sit on Sapele Road, just off Limit Road, on the G.R.A. side of Benin City. It is the rare address that is easy to reach and easy to sleep in. The main road carries you anywhere in the city in minutes, while G.R.A. keeps the traffic, the noise and the crowd one street away.

Every room has a proper bed, fast wifi, a smart TV and power that does not go off. One floor up, the restaurant runs from early morning until late. On the ground floor there is a bar a few steps off the reception, and a front desk that never closes.

    The restaurant, upstairs

    One floor up, with G.R.A. out the window

    The kitchen sits on the upper floor, so lunch comes with the tree line of G.R.A. through the glass instead of the car park. Banga, egusi and pepper soup share a menu with grills, pasta and a full English breakfast.

    Open from 7:00am to 11:00pm, with room service running through the night. Tell us about allergies or a special menu when you book and the kitchen will plan for it.

    The bar is a separate room on the ground floor, a few steps from reception. Different room, different mood: somewhere to sit with a drink after a long day without walking back into a dining room.

    If you would rather not go up, the bar sits on the ground floor a few steps off the reception. Same drinks list, no stairs.

    The address

    A street away from the noise

    Sapele Road is one of the main arteries of Benin City. Limit Road turns you into G.R.A., where the streets are wide, the compounds are gated and the evenings are quiet. You get the connection of the main road and the calm of the reserve at the same time.

    Ring Road, the banks and the government offices are a short drive out. The airport is closer still. Send us your flight details and a car will be waiting.
